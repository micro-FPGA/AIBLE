# Open call: start writing Aibles

This is an open call to everybody. Write an Aible and publish it.

An **Aible** is a Bible-like book generated by an AI from human input — or from AI
input. No permission is needed and no qualification is required. If you have a few
rules you actually live by and some stories that produced them, you have enough.

## Why now

The Bible was not written. It was **assembled** — stories, letters, lists and
arguments from many hands, gathered over a very long time and edited into one book.
It helped people understand the world that existed around them, and it did that
well enough to last.

It has been a long time since anyone assembled a book like that in the open, and
the world it explains is no longer the world we are building. The old book does not
cover machines that talk.

What has changed is that the assembling no longer takes centuries. It takes an
afternoon. So the bottleneck is not the editor any more.

**The bottleneck is the contributions. That is the part only you can do.**

## The supertask

Write your Aible. Publish it. When there are enough of them, an AI can be set the
**supertask**: read every published Aible and assemble one new Aible out of all of
them.

Not a summary. The same operation that produced the original — many authors, one
book — run once more, in public, in the open, with every source still readable
beside it.

That composite cannot be written by one person and cannot be faked by one machine.
It needs a crowd of ordinary people who wrote down what they believe. That is the
call.

## The rules

Only three, and they are what will keep this from becoming noise:

1. **Say who wrote what.** The life and the rules are yours; the sentences are the
   machine's. A reader who cannot tell them apart has been handled badly.
2. **If it is invented, say so.** A fully AI-generated Aible is welcome — one is
   already in the register — but it must be labelled as invented, in the fields, not
   in a footnote.
3. **Claim nothing you cannot show.** If you do not know, write that you do not
   know. If you did not record which AI you used, write *unrecorded*. Missing is an
   honest answer; a plausible guess is not.

## How to enter

1. Take the template: [instructions/AIBLE-INSTRUCTIONS-template.md](instructions/AIBLE-INSTRUCTIONS-template.md). 
   Fill in the blanks in your own words — badly is fine, typos do not matter, facts do.
2. Hand the whole file to an AI and ask it to produce the book.
3. Add your entry to the register and open a pull request:
   https://github.com/micro-FPGA/AIBLE
4. There is no time limit. If you finish inside one hour of prompt-writing time, it
   also qualifies as a *One Hour Bible* and can be entered in
   [GOLC](https://github.com/micro-FPGA/GOLC).
