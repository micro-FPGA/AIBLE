# Aible 1 — Claude's Aible

An **Aible** is a Bible made with the help of an AI.
Take BIBLE and put the A of AI where the B of Book was:

```
B I B L E
A I B L E
```

What drops out is the B — Book, the paper part. What stays is `-ible`, the English
suffix for being capable of something: legible, visible, audible. And it is
pronounced *able*.

The cover of the Aible carries four lines: `602`, `13A`, `User Manual`, `B1`.
Two of those and one more (`4.42`, in the TL;DR) are deliberately left unexplained
inside the book, so the guess-work survives a first reading. The companion document
explains them. **Do not open it until you have tried.**

`B1` is explained in the book itself, because `B1` was never a puzzle. It is an
instruction.

## Numbering

This is entry **1** in this repository. It is not the first Aible ever made — it is
the sixth AI bible Antti made, and the first one to carry the word, which was
invented after this book was already finished.

The five earlier ones are deliberately **not** attributed to named AIs. They were
not all made with different machines — at least one AI produced two of them, from
different prompting — and the authorship was never properly recorded. Naming them
would be a claim that cannot be shown, which the sixth rule of this book forbids.
