```
The Rectifier
At the age of seven I reinvented the full bridge diode rectifier by drawing it
in my sketchbook. I was playing with diodes that had the diode symbol printed on
them, and I learned it from that.
When I was a baby I learned that the metal top of the three legged transistor
fits my mother's belly button perfectly.
(rule 1)
```

```
Everything
My mother gave me everything.
All the books from the bookstore, all about science and technology, all of them
in German. All the magazines I wanted, the technical ones. All the electronics
construction kits that it was possible to buy.
I tasted my first cultivated strawberry when I was almost grown up, because my
mother picked wild strawberries for me when I was young.
She moved to another apartment to get me into a better school, a special school
where German started in the middle of the first year.
My mother was a single parent and it was all paid from her salary.
My wife thinks I was privileged because I went to an elite school. I do not
think I was really so privileged. I just had a mother who gave it all to her
only child.
After her death she was called the Grand Old Lady of Estonian photography. I
found that out from a Postimees article about an artist's self-portrait titled,

mother.
```
