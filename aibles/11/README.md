## What happened

In 2026 Antti Lukats opened an X account under the name Hope A. Freedom and
posted one thing on it:

```
@GretaThunberg

Do you have any use for:
* some land on an island with whistling sands
* small appartment close downtown in capital city
* small house+some land?
All yours if the answer is: YES
```

There were no replies. No acknowledgement, no refusal, almost certainly no human
being who read it. The offer is no longer valid; some of the land has since been
sold for other purposes. The typo is reproduced because the book reproduces it.

## Why this one is different from the rest

Aibles 2 to 8 invent a person and write their book. Aible 9 is Thierry Ehrmann's
own. Aible 10 invents a persona for a real living stranger and writes only from
public facts.

This one is the first where **the rules are not the author's.**

He was asked for them and declined — *"make up the rules!"* — so the seven in
this book were read off his acts by the AI that wrote it. That is stated on the
third page of the book, in the chapter about the machine, and again in the
chapter the machine writes about itself. It is not a creed he holds. He wrote
rules once, in 2001, and those appear in his own words.

## The seven

```
1. Make the offer anyway.
2. Ask in public.
3. One word is enough.
4. Keep the joke in.
5. Giving is harder than keeping.
6. Let it expire.
7. The gesture is the thing, not the answer.
```

## Five shekels

The condition attached to the two island properties was that Greta keep one and
make her best attempt to sell the other to Richard Branson for **5 SEK**, paid
in full, in advance, blind, without seeing it.

Asked why, the author first said it was just a funny amount, and the AI was
about to record it as meaningless — the template forbids assigning meaning to a
number the author has not explained. He then gave the real answer: **SEK is
close to shekel.**

Five shekels is the price of *pidyon haben*, the redemption of the firstborn son
([Numbers 18:16](https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/586454/jewish/All-About-the-Five-Shekels.htm)) —
the father pays five shekels of silver to a kohen and the child is released from
being wholly dedicated. By one traditional reckoning it is also what Joseph
fetched from his brothers.

So the island is not being sold to a billionaire for a joke sum. It is being
**redeemed**, at the canonical price. The island came into the family the same
way: the author's mother bought the whole estate for 300 rubles, blind, without
seeing it.

## 2001

The spine of the book is a text file the author published in Tallinn on
21 August 2001 and which is still in the Internet Archive:

```
1 No Promises.
2 Keep Promises.
3 Give away what you do not need.
4 Do what you want to do.
5 Be Happy.
```

The same file contains a FAQ, and the FAQ says:

```
Q: Are you following R.O.L. ?
A: No. (see also rule #4)
```

Rule 3 was written twenty-five years before it was attempted, by a man who
recorded in the same document that he was not following it.

[RulesOfLife.txt, archived 23 January 2002](https://web.archive.org/web/20020123150519/http://case2000.com/R.O.L/RulesOfLife.txt)

## The questions that changed the book

The AI asked before writing, and three answers changed what got written:

1. **Was it real, or a gesture?** Real. Without that answer the whole book could
   have been written as performance, and would have been false.
2. **Why 5 SEK?** The second answer, not the first. See above.
3. **What are your rules?** He would not give any. Hence the seven, and hence
   this section.

These are recorded inside the book as well as here, because a register whose
claim is provenance should show how its entries were actually made.

## What is not in it

The author cleared material from his own unpublished *Antti Bible* for use here,
and most of the stories come from it. Two things were left out anyway: a passage
about his body and his health, which is not this book's business, and his
unpublished prose — the facts are used, the sentences are not, so his own book
keeps its own words.

Richard Branson was never approached. Greta Thunberg has still not replied — not
to this, and not to the calendar the author gave her in 2019, which made that
year 0 Anno Greta and is why this book is dated 7 AG.
